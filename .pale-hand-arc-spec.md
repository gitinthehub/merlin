# "The Pale Hand" — the arc spec

The owner's design for the **second Oronath arc**, written down as the content contract for roadmap
item **2.9**. The source of truth for the story is the owner's own GM prompt, committed verbatim as
[`.pale-hand-source.txt`](.pale-hand-source.txt). This file says how that prompt becomes a
**validated Oronath arc** — every scene, choice, roll, road and ending of the prompt, mapped onto
the engine's real mechanics — and it records, item by item, what the engine cannot carry and how
each of those things is carried instead.

The engine's validator is the gate: `arc.js`'s `validateArc` must accept the built artefact with
**zero errors**, and the build must be refused if it does not.

## Premise

Bridgeport, a river city of tar-black canals and coal soot, ruled by Magistrate Corvane, whose Ash
Guard burns the homes of "sympathizers". **Jin**, a courier for the resistance, is handed the stolen
**Ledger** — Corvane's book of orders — and must get it to Commander Thorne's camp in the northern
hills before **the Pale Hand** (Aldric, Corvane's assassin) reaches her first.

Five chapters, five endings, one protagonist, three threads. Tone: vivid, cinematic, second person,
short punchy lines, small telling details, no rambling. Clear cause → effect → consequence.

## Cast and the threads they must close

| Character | id | Traits (id · label · difficulty · advantage) | Thread | What closing it means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jin** | `jin` | `sharp-mind` · Sharp Mind · −2 · no · **·** `steady-voice` · Steady Voice · 0 · **yes** · **·** `clumsy-hands` · Clumsy Hands · +1 · no · **·** `fighting` · Fighting · −1 · no | `jin-ledger` — **the Ledger** | Jin learns what the Ledger's Millbrook page means (the Hand's mother is on the burn order) — or the ending settles the book's fate. |
| **Wren** | `wren` | `thorn-archers` · Thornwood archers · −2 · yes · **·** `deserter` · Ash Guard deserter · 0 · no | `wren-band` — **the deserters** | Wren's band has a side in this war. |
| **Thorne** | `thorne` | `commander` · Commander of the resistance · −2 · no · **·** `scarred` · Scarred, and twice shot · 0 · no | `thorne-resistance` — **the resistance** | The resistance holds, or it does not. |

- Trait `difficulty` is added to the DC by the engine (`effectiveDc = baseDc + modifier`), so a
  negative number makes a check **easier** and a positive number makes it **harder**. `clumsy-hands`
  is the only positive one, because Clumsy Hands is the character's stated weakness.
- `steady-voice` carries `advantage: true`: an average skill that does not crack under pressure —
  two dice, keep the best. This is the arc's one expression of Jin's "average but steady" voice.
- **Wren and Thorne do not roll.** The engine's choice set is static and every choice is offered on
  every path, so a Wren-actor choice would be offered on the Toll Bridge road, where she has never
  been met. Her archers are therefore narrated *inside* Jin's roads, and her joining is `wren-band`
  closing. See "What the engine cannot carry" below.
- Thread labels are the source prompt's status bar, made visible: the Oronath surface already
  renders the run's thread list under each chapter, so **ALLIES and CLUE are live run state** (a
  thread that closes mid-run reads `— closed` from that point on), while WOUNDS, TIME and SILVER are
  carried by the prose (below).
- Every ending must resolve all three threads (`endings[].resolves` lists all three on all five
  endings), which is what the validator's `unresolved-thread` rule requires on **every path**.

## Chapters, choices, rolls and roads (the whole graph)

Rolls: `d20` with a `tier` (`easy` 8 / `medium` 11 / `hard` 14 / `veryHard` 16) and `tags` naming
the traits the check leans on. The `(eff N)` column is the effective DC after the trait modifier —
the number the source prompt's DC is mapped onto. Five chapters (the validator's maximum), because
the prompt's Chapter 3A and Chapter 3B are two different roads that must both exist.

`✓` in the last column means the road also closes a thread.

### Chapter 1 — `lantern-quarter` · "The Lantern Quarter"

**Setting:** Bridgeport at night, rain on the rooftops; the back room of a candle shop, where Vera —
a resistance fence with ink-stained fingers — shoves the Ledger, and a smoke bomb, into Jin's hands.
Bells clang; the Ash Guard is sweeping the quarter door by door, torch on every corner.
**Objective:** get the Ledger out of the Lantern Quarter before the sweep reaches Vera's door.

| # | Choice id | Label | Roll | s → | f → | c → | b → |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `jin-rooftops` | Take the rooftops and go over the city wall | d20 hard, `clumsy-hands` (eff 15) | `crosshaven` | `crosshaven` | `toll-bridge` | `crosshaven` |
| 2 | `jin-sewers` | Take the sewers out to the river grate | d20 medium, `sharp-mind` (eff 9) | `crosshaven` | `crosshaven` | `toll-bridge` | `crosshaven` |
| 3 | `jin-bluff-gate` | Bluff the gate guards as a drunk dockworker | d20 medium, `steady-voice` (eff 11) | `crosshaven` | `crosshaven` | `toll-bridge` | `crosshaven` |
| 4 | `jin-watch-patrols` | Watch the patrols from Vera's window and find the seam | d20 medium, `sharp-mind` (eff 9) | `crosshaven` | `crosshaven` | `toll-bridge` | `crosshaven` |

- Every successful road may narrate the source's own rewards: over the wall unseen != a night ahead
  (`jin-rooftops` success), "you overhear *the Hand's taking the north road*" (`jin-bluff-gate`
  success), a guard reading a letter sealed with the word **Millbrook** (`jin-watch-patrols`
  success — the seeded mention the source asks to be flagged).
- `jin-watch-patrols`'s source failure ("you wait too long, then pick 1, 2 or 3") cannot be a retry —
  see "What the engine cannot carry" #2.
- **Complication roads (`c`) all lead to `toll-bridge`:** on a natural 1 you are flushed out of the
  city at a run and come onto the north road *without* the market stop. This is the source's "+cost,
  story moves on" shape and it is a genuinely different road: no gear, no mare, no rumour.
- **Bonus roads (`b`):** the natural 20 is "success plus a small bonus" — Vera's cousin tosses a
  healing potion at the gate (the source's own natural-20 rule).

### Chapter 2 — `crosshaven` · "Crosshaven Market"

**Setting:** dawn, two days later, the last market town before the northern hills: spice smoke,
goats, a toothless fortune-teller, and Bram, whose beard is like steel wool. His stall's prices are
the source's, listed in the setting text: Healing Potion 25 silver, Smoke Bombs (2) 20, Longsword
35, **Ash** the fast stubborn gray mare 60, Rope and Climbing Gear 15; one haggle allowed.
**Objective:** leave Crosshaven with what the hills will not sell you, on one road or the other.

| # | Choice id | Label | Roll | s → | f → | c → | b → |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `jin-bram-main` | Haggle Bram for rope and a potion, then take the main road | d20 medium, `steady-voice` (eff 11) | `toll-bridge` | `toll-bridge` | `thornwood` | `toll-bridge` |
| 2 | `jin-bram-wood` | Buy the climbing rope and take the Thornwood Pass | d20 hard, `sharp-mind` (eff 12) | `thornwood` | `thornwood` | `toll-bridge` | `thornwood` |
| 3 | `jin-fortune` | Pay the toothless fortune-teller, then ride the main road | d20 medium, `sharp-mind` (eff 9) | `toll-bridge` | `toll-bridge` | `thornwood` | `toll-bridge` |
| 4 | `jin-no-stop` | Take the pass without stopping for anyone | d20 medium, `clumsy-hands` (eff 12) | `thornwood` | `thornwood` | `toll-bridge` | `thornwood` |

- The fork is real: the main road (`toll-bridge`) is the patrolled one, the pass (`thornwood`) is
  the fast one with bandits and wolves in it.
- `jin-bram-main` carries the source's single haggle (DC 13 → the medium-voice roll); `jin-bram-wood`
  is the rope purchase, which is what the source says makes the bridge climb easier — narrate it on
  the roads, do not try to change the bridge chapter's DCs.
- `jin-fortune` success names **Millbrook** and the man who has been asking about it — a *lead*, not
  the secret. It must **not** close `jin-ledger`: the secret is who lives there, and that is
  Chapter 3's to give.
- Complication roads flip the road (a bailiff's whistle sends you up the wood road; the wood road's
  own complication spills you back onto the flat) — a natural 1 in the market costs you the road you
  chose, not the story.

### Chapter 3A — `toll-bridge` · "The Toll Bridge" (main road)

**Setting:** a stone bridge over a roaring gorge; an Ash Guard checkpoint where a sergeant holds
travelers' faces up against wanted sketches, and one sketch looks a lot like Jin.
**Objective:** get the Ledger across the gorge.

| # | Choice id | Label | Roll | s → | f → | c → | b → |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `jin-bribe` | Put silver in the sergeant's hand | d20 easy, `steady-voice` (eff 8) | `the-ford` ✓ | `end-captured` | `thornwood` | `the-ford` ✓ |
| 2 | `jin-checkpoint-bluff` | Bluff the sergeant with a dockworker's accent | d20 hard, `steady-voice` (eff 14) | `the-ford` ✓ | `the-ford` | `thornwood` | `the-ford` ✓ |
| 3 | `jin-under-bridge` | Climb under the bridge on the beams | d20 hard, `clumsy-hands` (eff 15) | `the-ford` ✓ | `the-ford` | `end-captured` | `the-ford` ✓ |
| 4 | `jin-fight-checkpoint` | Draw the short sword | d20 veryHard, `fighting` (eff 15) | `the-ford` ✓ | `the-ford` | `thornwood` | `the-ford` ✓ |

- ✓ = the road closes `jin-ledger` (the source's own CLUE): nailed to the bridge post is the notice
  **"Millbrook: clearance in thirty days. By order of Corvane."**, and you hold it against the
  Ledger's page, where the margin note reads **"The Hand's mother still lives there. Do NOT inform
  him."** Both strings must appear verbatim on the road that closes the thread.
- The failed bribe is the source's "he pockets the money and calls for backup" — here it *is* the
  capture road (ENDING E), which is where the source's wound accumulation would have sent a
  bleeding courier. The climb's fall into the shallows is the second capture road.
- Bluff and fight failures still get you across, bleeding and with the checkpoint behind you
  (the source's own outcomes).
- Complications take you off the road into the Thornwood (`thornwood`) — and note the graph must
  never run the other way: `thornwood` has **no** road back to `toll-bridge`, or the validator's
  cycle rule fails.

### Chapter 3B — `thornwood` · "The Thornwood Ambush" (the pass)

**Setting:** ancient oaks strangled by thorn vines, no birdsong; an overturned merchant cart blocks
the trail, too neat to be an accident; an arrow thunks into a tree an inch from Jin's head, and a
woman's voice comes down from the branches: **"Horse. Weapon. Walk away."** — Wren, who leads five
Ash Guard deserters.
**Objective:** get out of the wood with the Ledger.

| # | Choice id | Label | Roll | s → | f → | c → | b → |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `jin-still` | Stay still, count the archers, and watch | d20 medium, `sharp-mind` (eff 9) | `the-ford` | `the-ford` | `end-captured` | `the-ford` |
| 2 | `jin-talk` | Talk: tell them what the Ledger holds | d20 hard, `steady-voice` (eff 14) | `the-ford` ✓✓ | `the-ford` | `end-captured` | `the-ford` ✓✓ |
| 3 | `jin-fight-band` | Fight them | d20 veryHard, `fighting` (eff 15) | `the-ford` | `the-ford` | `end-hand-vanishes` | `the-ford` |
| 4 | `jin-smoke-bomb` | Smoke bomb and ride | d20 easy, `clumsy-hands` (eff 9) | `the-ford` | `the-ford` | `end-hand-vanishes` | `the-ford` |

- ✓✓ = the road closes **both** `wren-band` and `jin-ledger`: Wren's jaw tightens, she joins, and
  she says **"The Hand came through yesterday. Didn't touch us. Asked about Millbrook, of all
  places."** — the source's Chapter 3B CLUE, from her mouth instead of a second roll.
- `jin-still`'s source reward ("your next check this chapter is 3 easier, then choose again") is a
  retry, which the engine forbids; it becomes its own road (you count four archers, spot Wren, and
  she lets you walk) — see "What the engine cannot carry" #2.
- `jin-smoke-bomb` is the source's automatic success: the easiest check in the arc (eff 9), and its
  cost is the source's own — Wren can never join, so `wren-band` stays open and the ending says so.
- `jin-talk`'s natural 20 gives the white-fletched arrows, **"The Hand will know these. He trained
  me."** — the source's Chapter 4 bonus, carried as prose.
- Complications here end the run early on a road the player chose: two capture roads
  (`end-captured`) and two "you lose the night, and the Hand is already gone" roads
  (`end-hand-vanishes`) — the source's cost, not a retry.

### Chapter 4 — `the-ford` · "The Hand at the Ford"

**Setting:** Thorne's camp in a pine hollow above the river ford; fog coming off the water as the
camp dogs start barking; a sentry lying unconscious but unharmed; the Hand already inside. Thorne is
down and the Hand stands over her with a thin blade out and a white glove gleaming in the firelight.
He speaks: **"Courier. You carry something that belongs to the Magistrate. Set it down and walk
away. I don't kill people who aren't on my list."**
**Objective:** get Thorne and the Ledger out of the ford alive.

| # | Choice id | Label | Roll | s → | f → | c → | b → |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `jin-fight-hand` | Draw the short sword and go at him in the fog | d20 veryHard, `fighting` (eff 15) | `end-hand-falls` | `end-captured` | `end-hand-vanishes` | `end-hand-falls` |
| 2 | `jin-show-ledger` | Show him the Millbrook page and tell him about his mother | d20 medium, `steady-voice` (eff 11) | `end-hand-turns` | `end-hand-vanishes` | `end-captured` | `end-hand-turns` |
| 3 | `jin-offer-ledger` | Offer the Ledger in exchange for Thorne's life | d20 easy, `steady-voice` (eff 8) | `end-bargain` | `end-bargain` | `end-captured` | `end-bargain` |
| 4 | `jin-smoke-thorne` | Smoke bomb, then drag Thorne out of the tent | d20 medium, `clumsy-hands` (eff 12) | `end-hand-vanishes` | `end-captured` | `end-hand-falls` | `end-hand-vanishes` |

- `jin-show-ledger` is the arc's best ending and its difficulty is the source's **with-the-clue**
  number (DC 11). The engine cannot switch a check's DC by a flag (see #3 below): the
  **without-the-clue** case is carried by the failure road's text — and failure is the source's own
  outcome there anyway ("he doesn't believe you, but he hesitates long enough for Thorne to escape",
  ENDING C).
- `jin-offer-ledger` is the source's automatic success; failure costs the bargain dearly (he takes
  the Ledger *and* the purse) but still lands on ENDING D, so both roads are the bargain.
- The smoke-bomb road's complication is ENDING B: the smoke blows wrong, you drag Thorne into the
  water, and Wren's arrows — or your blade in the shallows — finish it.
- The fight's failure is ENDING E (the source's "+2 WOUNDS" with nobody left to pull Jin out), and
  its complication is ENDING C (Thorne drags Jin clear; the Hand walks into the fog).

### Endings (five, one per source ending — ids are the contract)

| Ending id | Title | Source | Threads | The images and lines it must land |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `end-hand-turns` | **The Hand Turns** | A (best) | all three | The Millbrook page read twice; the glove pulled off; a shaking hand; **"She's still there. My mother."**; he kneels to Thorne; the white glove curling in the campfire. |
| `end-hand-falls` | **The Hand Falls** | B | all three | Steel in the fog; Aldric drops into the ford; the white glove drifting downstream; the resistance holds, but Corvane will send someone worse and no one warns Millbrook. |
| `end-hand-vanishes` | **The Hand Vanishes** | C | all three | Thorne lives; the Hand melts into the fog; a victory with a bill attached — **Jin's name is on the list**. |
| `end-bargain` | **The Courier's Bargain** | D | all three | The Ledger handed over without a word; the purge list back in Corvane's hands; Thorne's hand on Jin's shoulder: **"We'll get it back."** |
| `end-captured` | **Captured** | E | all three | A cell in Bridgeport; a white glove folded on the stone floor outside the bars; a voice from the dark: **"You nearly had it, courier."** |

Every ending text must stand alone: it is the *same string* on every path that reaches it, so it may
never refer to something only one road did (no "if you met Wren…"). Epilogue voice, 4–6 sentences,
second person, and each one states what happened to the Ledger, to Wren's band and to the resistance
— that is what "resolves all three threads" means on the page.

## What the engine cannot carry, and how each thing is carried instead

The source prompt is written for a human GM holding a status bar. The Oronath engine has dice, a
chapter graph, real branch targets and per-run thread state — and **no counters and no conditional
choice sets**. Nothing below is a shortfall to be papered over with a fake mechanic; each item says
where the source's intent actually lands.

1. **WOUNDS / "at 3 wounds, ENDING E".** There is no wound counter and no vitals in `loop.js`'s run
   state, and the pass may not touch `loop.js` or `persist.js`. Capture therefore stops being an
   accumulation and becomes a **road**: ENDING E is reached from `jin-bribe` failure,
   `jin-under-bridge` complication, `jin-still` complication, `jin-talk` complication,
   `jin-fight-hand` failure, `jin-show-ledger` complication, `jin-offer-ledger` complication and
   `jin-smoke-thorne` failure. Wounds are the *price* on the failure roads, narrated, and the source's
   "restart from Chapter 1 with TIME = Ahead" is the surface's existing **New Run**.
2. **Retries the prompt allows ("choose again", "then pick 1, 2 or 3").** The engine's rule is
   "failure creates a new consequence, never a retry", and the validator rejects a self-next. Each
   such moment becomes a distinct road instead: waiting too long in Chapter 1 is the complication
   road to `toll-bridge`; watching the ambush in Chapter 3B is its own road to `the-ford`; a failed
   bribe at the bridge is the capture road.
3. **CLUE switching a DC (11 with the clue, 17 without).** A check's difficulty is static per choice.
   The clue is therefore carried as **run state the player can see** — the `jin-ledger` thread closes
   on the roads that actually learn it (the bridge notice held against the page; Wren's news) — and
   as prose: `jin-show-ledger` is rolled at the with-the-clue number, and its failure road is the
   source's guessing case. `jin-fortune` in Chapter 2 plants the name without closing the thread.
4. **TIME (Ahead / Even / Behind) selecting the Chapter 4 scene.** Three variants of one chapter
   would need three chapter nodes and the arc is already at the validator's five. Chapter 4 is the
   source's **EVEN** scene (arrive as the dogs bark, sentry down, the Hand already inside, no prep);
   "Ahead" and "Behind" are the *roads you took to get there* (the pass is the fast road, the bridge
   is the patrolled one, the market costs a day) and are narrated on the roads into the Ford and
   inside the endings. The prep options (a trap, Wren's archers) live in the fight choice's road
   texts and in the white-fletched arrows the natural 20 gives.
5. **The shop (buy any mix, one haggle).** The engine has no shop UI. Bram's stall lives in the
   chapter's setting text with the source's exact prices, the market choices are what Jin spends on,
   and the source's single haggle (DC 13) is `jin-bram-main`'s roll. Rope is what the source says
   makes the bridge climb easier — narrated, not a DC change.
6. **"Or something else."** The arc is four written choices per chapter; there is no free-text road,
   and nothing in the source's outcomes is dropped for that — every written outcome is a road.
7. **ALLIES and CLUE are the two the engine *can* carry**, and they are carried as the visible
   thread list under each chapter (`wren-band` closed = Wren joined; `jin-ledger` closed = Jin knows
   about Millbrook). SILVER and ITEMS are prose: they change no check in this arc.

## What the artefact must satisfy (the validator is the gate)

- Arc `id` `pale-hand`, `version` 1, `title` "The Pale Hand", plus a one-line `blurb` and a `pitch`
  for the story picker (extra keys are ignored by the validator and are what the picker shows).
- **Five chapters, exactly the five ids above**, in that order (`lantern-quarter` first — it is the
  start), each with a `setting`, an `objective`, and the choices in the tables above.
- **Every choice carries all four consequences** — `success`, `failure`, `complication`, `bonus` —
  each with non-blank `text` and a `next`, matching the shipped arc's convention and its test.
- **`complication.next` differs from `failure.next` on every choice** (a complication is a road,
  never a retry), and no consequence points at its own chapter.
- Every thread is resolved on **every** path: all three threads in every ending's `resolves`, with
  the mid-run closures (`wren-band`, `jin-ledger`) as listed above.
- No dangling `next`, no cycles, no unreachable chapter or ending; every chapter can reach an ending.
- Four choices per chapter — the source's own count — and the source's DCs mapped onto the engine's
  tiers exactly as the `(eff N)` column says.
- The load-bearing lines from the source appear **verbatim** somewhere in the arc: Vera's
  "You're faster than a letter and dumber than a coward. Perfect. Go."; the Hand's "Courier. You
  carry something that belongs to the Magistrate. Set it down and walk away. I don't kill people who
  aren't on my list."; Wren's "Horse. Weapon. Walk away." and her Millbrook line; "She's still
  there. My mother."; the bridge notice and the Ledger's margin note; "The Hand will know these. He
  trained me."; "You nearly had it, courier."; and Thorne's "We'll get it back."
- Written as `arcs/pale-hand.json` (source) and shipped as `arcs/pale-hand.js`, built by
  `node tools/build-arc.js --from arcs/pale-hand.json --out arcs/pale-hand.js` — the **keyless
  path**. No API key, no network, no build step, no ES modules; the bundle is a plain IIFE a
  `file://` page can load with a `<script>` tag.

## How it ships

Same pipeline as the first arc (roadmap 2.6/2.7): author the source JSON, validate and bundle
keyless, commit the generated `.js`, push — Netlify publishes `.` with no build. The story picker
(roadmap 2.10) is what makes the second arc reachable: the Oronath surface lists every registered
bundle and the player chooses one at the start.
