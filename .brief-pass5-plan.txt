## Task — plan exactly three items

### 1.1 A die you can see — the headline requirement

Today there is no die. `ui.js` has `flashDie(text, success)` which writes the roll's **text** into a single `#die` div and toggles a `.tumbling` class for 600ms; `engine.js:formatRoll()` builds that string. The owner's requirement, verbatim: *"MAKE SURE that there is a cool dice roll animation or at least that it shows the dice in the faces up that it should (if d20 then at least show the d20 with the right top number showing)."*

Plan a real rendered polyhedral die component that always shows **the face that was actually rolled**:

- **d20** — a genuine d20 silhouette (icosahedron look: hexagonal outline with internal triangle edges), the rolled number upright and readable on the top/front face.
- **d10** — pentagonal-trapezohedron silhouette, showing its rolled value.
- **d6 / 2d6** — cube(s); 2d6 renders two dice plus the total.
- **Advantage** — two d20s rendered, the kept one highlighted, the dropped one dimmed, with the kept number on top.
- **Crits** — natural 20 / natural 1 called out on the die itself (not only in the text line).
- **Reduced motion** — `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the final face immediately, no tumble.
- Keep the existing equation line (e.g. `d20 rolled 14 + WITS 2 = 16 vs DC 12`) — the die is an addition, not a replacement for the maths.

This requires the engine to hand the UI the actual dice. Audit **every** roll site in `engine.js` — `formatRoll` callers and the damage rolls (`applyOption`, the combat resolvers, the save-check path, the flee path, the `1d6`/`2d6` damage rolls, the crit paths) — and specify a structured roll payload (e.g. `{ kind, dice, kept, dropped, total, stat, mod, dc, critSuccess, critFail }`) attached to each result/outcome without changing the printed text.
