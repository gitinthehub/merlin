## Task

**1.10 Tombstone line, as specified (S).** The card's death line must read like the owner's example: *"Pip, clerk. Lectured to death in the throne room. Owed 3 gold."* — i.e. hero (short name/role), **what killed them and where** (cause + the location it happened in, not the ending's title), and **gold owed**. Plan how each part is derived from real state, and how the existing per-cause epitaph writing is kept.

**1.9 The Count reviews your visit (M).** A **per-run** review in the Count's voice, built from what that run actually did — the owner's example: *"Paid the toll. Did not tip the bat. Called me the other thing once, in the marsh, where you thought I couldn't hear. Two stars."* It must be assembled from flags and counts the engine already tracks (audit `data.js`/`engine.js` for them), shown on every ending, and printed on the share card. Additive to the existing cross-run memory, which is a different feature.

**1.11 Oswald's guest book + the castle-gate checkpoint (S–M).** Reframe the Fates Ledger as **Oswald's guest book**: unfound entries as blank lines with a hint in **Oswald's** voice (the owner's example: *"A guest who was polite to the bat. We have not had one."*), plus a couple of secret entries. Separately: **a checkpoint at the castle gate** so a death costs a minute instead of the whole run — plan what is snapshotted, when, where the restore is offered, and how it interacts with the death still being recorded in the guest book.

**1.8 The Daily Curse (M).** A once-a-day run: the **same hero and the same dice for everyone that day**. Plan:
- a **seed** derived from the calendar date, exposed to the player, and a deterministic RNG (injectable, e.g. mulberry32) replacing direct `Math.random()` at every roll site so a seed reproduces a run exactly;
- the day's hero chosen by the seed, with the normal free character select still available outside Daily Curse mode;
- a Wordle-style share line in exactly this shape: `MERLIN #41 🧛 Pip · 🎲 20 · 7 · 2 · 17 · Ending: The Correction` — plus one line from the Count;
- how the run number ("#41") is derived and what happens when the same player replays the day.

**1.12 Portraits must be prominent (S).** On the character-select screen each hero's painted portrait is currently a 40px thumbnail, which does not answer "what does this character look like". Plan each hero as a proper card image — full width of its card, roughly 160–240px tall on desktop and 96–120px on mobile — with name, role, stats and voice line beneath it, and fix the card text collapsing into a ragged narrow column at ~800px. In the character sheet head the portrait should read clearly at about 72px, keeping the glyph fallback.
