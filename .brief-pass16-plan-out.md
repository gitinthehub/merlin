## 5. The new Fallout-themed wasteland arc

**Premise.** Cinder Mile is a rail town in the dry country that still paints its mile-posts. After dark, a relay in the hills speaks for one hour. Nim is trying to learn whether the posts are a map. Pell keeps a hand crank in the switch house and wants tonight’s hour to go out. Maro is on a ladder in the yard, repainting a number she calls close enough for a town. No Vault-Tec, vault numbers, Pip-Boy, Nuka-Cola, Brotherhood of Steel, Deathclaw, Super Mutant, or any other product faction. Trade is water and lamp oil. The voice is wry and specific.

**Titles.** Use **Cinder Mile**, id `cinder-mile`. It names a place that can hold all three threads and borrows no product title. **The Painted Mile** names the posts and loses the town. **Tonight’s Hour** names Pell’s question and spends the ending in the title.

**Cast and threads.** Ending `resolves` on all three endings is `["nim-map", "pell-hour", "maro-posts"]`. Prose has to stay true on routes that never enter the switch house and never take Maro’s choice. Pell rolls only in `switch-house`. Maro rolls only in `cinder-yard`. The relay chapters are Nim alone, because `relay-tower` is also reachable by a cached crank on the flat.

| Character | id | Traits (id, label, difficulty, advantage) | Thread | Closing line that is true on every route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nim | `nim` | `road-sense` Road Sense −2 no; `light-hands` Light Hands 0 **yes**; `bad-knee` Bad Knee +1 no | `nim-map` “the tower on the posts” | The posts were a way toward the relay, or they were not, and that question is settled by which ending you are in. |
| Pell | `pell` | `solder` Solder −2 no; `night-shift` Night Shift 0 **yes** | `pell-hour` “tonight’s hour” | The hour goes out, or it does not. The switch house may stay dark. |

Derived cast line: `Nim, Pell, Maro`.

**Chapters (5).** No edge points backward.

1. `cinder-yard` — The Painted Yard. Maro on the ladder, Pell’s light in the switch house, paint still wet. Objective: pick a way toward the voice before the paint dries.
2. `switch-house` — The Switch House. Pell and the crank. Only from yard roads that go inside. Objective: leave with a crank that turns, or without one.
3. `salt-flat` — The Salt Flat. No Pell choice. Objective: cross on the numbers, or lose them.
4. `relay-tower` — The Relay. Crank in hand or a crank cached in a post. Objective: decide what the hour is for.
5. `relay-tower-dark` — The Relay, Uncranked. Housing empty. Objective: climb, read, or walk off.

**Endings (3).**

- `end-hour` — The Hour Goes Out. Reached only from a relay chapter. Spine: the relay speaks for one hour; the switch house can stay dark; the numbers are still on the posts.
- `end-static` — A Voice in Salt. Reached only from a relay chapter. Spine: the relay is a loop, and tonight’s hour does not go out.
- `end-miles` — Wrong Miles. Reached from the flat and from both relays. Spine: you leave the mile; tonight’s hour does not go out; the numbers stay where they were painted.
