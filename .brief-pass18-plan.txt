## The problem to solve

The arc reads as atmosphere without motive. Nim, Pell and Maro want things the player is never
told plainly; the choices are errands rather than decisions, so a failure costs a chapter and not
anything the player cares about. The owner's diagnosis is that the story does not make sense.

Rewrite the arc's story data so that:

1. **Motivations are explicit and specific.** By the end of chapter one the player knows what each
   of the three wants, why they want it now, and what they are afraid of losing. Put those wants in
   `pitch` (one line per character, the shape the current file already uses) and let every chapter's
   `setting` and `objective` carry the pressure rather than the scenery.
2. **Every choice is a soul-crushing trade, not an errand.** A good choice here costs something the
   player wants to keep: save the hour and lose the map, keep the promise and lose the town, clear
   your name and burn the only copy. No choice may be obviously correct, and no road may be free.
   Two goods against each other, never good against bad.
3. **Failure opens a road with its own cost.** Never a retry, never a flat "you lose". The engine
   already routes to a different node; make that node something the player now has to live with.

## Hard rules — the validator is the gate

`arc.js`'s `validateArc` must return zero errors. Concretely:

- 3–6 chapters (the current arc has five; staying at five or six is fine), 2–4 choices per chapter.
- Every choice needs `roll.die` (`d20` with tier `easy|medium|hard|veryHard`, or `d10`/`2d6` with a
  numeric `dc`) and a `tags` array. A choice cannot be roll-free. "Automatic" means the easiest tier.
- Every consequence (`success`, `failure`, and `complication`/`bonus` where used) needs `text`,
  `next`, and `resolves`.
- `complication.next` must differ from `failure.next`. No dangling `next`, no self-`next`, no cycles,
  every chapter reachable from the first, every ending reachable, and **every ending must resolve all
  three threads**.
- Keep the arc's identity: `id` stays `cinder-mile`, `title` stays `Cinder Mile`. The door's
  `stories.js` row and the front-door card depend on the id, and the card prints `blurb`,
  `chapterCount` and `cast` — so keep the three-character cast shape and keep the cast names
  Nim, Pell and Maro, and keep the three thread ids (`nim-map`, `pell-hour`, `maro-posts`).
- No real product names, no franchise trade dress. The existing test asserts a denylist
  (`Vault-Tec`, `Pip-Boy`, `Nuka-Cola`, `Brotherhood of Steel`, `Deathclaw`, `Super Mutant`) is
  absent; that assertion stays.

## The deferred review findings this rewrite must also fix

A QA review of this arc found the following and they were deferred to exactly this kind of data pass
(see `.qa/qa-pass16-triage.md`, items D2, D3, D5). All three are in scope now:

- **D2 — roads that pay off backwards.** `nim-climb-dark`'s `bonus` goes to `end-static` while its
  plain `success` goes to `end-hour`; `nim-cut-cable` and `nim-count-the-plate` take `end-hour` on
  their `complication` where `success` takes `end-static`; `nim-walk-off`'s complication is
  `end-hour`. Fix the ordering by DESTINATION, not by prose: for every choice, `bonus` must be at
  least as good as `success`, and `complication` must be worse than `success` and no better than
  `failure`. State that ordering in the plan per choice so it can be checked.
- **D3 — text and graph contradict each other.** The Uncranked chapter's `objective` promises a
  walk-off that none of its four choices offers (either give it the choice or change the objective,
  and say which). The crank `bonus`'s text contradicts the next chapter's "the housing is empty".
- **D5 — one character does everything.** Nim acts in 12 of the 14 choices; Pell in one, Maro in one;
  and the traits `Solder` and `Long Memory` are never rolled. The rewrite must spread the acting
  across all three characters (no character above roughly half the choices, each acting in at least
  three) and every trait on every character must be the `tags` of at least one choice.
