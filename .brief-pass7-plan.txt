## The product spec these modules must satisfy

- Complete story arcs are **pre-generated before gameplay begins**, not improvised turn by turn. An arc is 3–5 chapters; each chapter has a setting description, a core objective, 2–4 branching choices, and clear success/failure consequences.
- **Every meaningful action requires a roll.** Difficulty is d20-scaled (easy 8+, medium 11+, hard 14+, very hard 16+), with d10 for reaction/timing/chaos and 2d6 for force/teamwork/damage.
- **Failure must create a new consequence or branch — never a "try again".** A natural 1 is a **major complication**, not a plain miss; a max roll is a bonus success or story advantage.
- Character traits shift roll difficulty and can grant advantage ("advantage due to build"), and **all character arcs must resolve by the end of the story — no loose threads** (the validator already enforces this before play).
- Tone: cinematic, vivid, specific, visual. Clear cause → effect → consequence.

## Task — plan two deliverables

**2.4 Gameplay loop / UI.** A playable surface that presents the current chapter's setting and objective, its choices, and the roll results, and tracks progress through the arc. It must run an arc that has already passed the validator, resolve every choice through the real dice engine (never a prompted or faked roll), show the player the die that was actually rolled with the face that came up, branch on success/failure/complication rather than offering a retry, and reach a resolution where every character thread has closed.

**2.5 Persistence.** Save and resume an in-progress run: the arc id, the current chapter and choice history, dice log, character state, and enough RNG state that a resumed run continues its dice stream rather than replaying it.
